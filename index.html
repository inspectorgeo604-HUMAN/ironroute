FAQ

Common Questions

How often should a semi truck be aligned?

Most fleets align every 3–6 months or roughly every 50,000–60,000 miles. Owner-operators should align after any curb strike, suspension repair, or new tire install.

Can you align a truck on-site?

Yes. Our mobile equipment handles steer and drive axle alignment at your location. Full trailer alignment may require a follow-up depending on space.

Do you work with fleets?

Yes. We offer scheduled PM and priority dispatch for fleets of 5+ units, with consolidated monthly invoicing.
